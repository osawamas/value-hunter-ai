# VALUE HUNTER AI v519

The private Android app includes an IBM pilot using a user's own Alpha
Vantage daily key, Frankfurter USD/JPY reference rates and the verified
bundled IBM September 2026 SEC filing basis. Stale or pre-disclosure
financial data does not qualify. Valuations are estimates using dated
filing shares. The US scan has its own baseline and background job.
Live authentication and on-device notification delivery remain open.

SGX source research: EODHD offers a limited free EOD plan, but free
access to specific SGX tickers has not been established. Moomoo's
published quote permissions list SGX securities as unsupported. Tiger
OpenAPI describes separately purchased market data. Twelve Data's EOD
documentation lists Singapore under licensing or availability restrictions.
No free personal SGX route has been confirmed.

DataCedar is the leading follow-up candidate. Its Singapore listing and
S63 pages describe a free key for raw daily bars, but the general pricing
page describes a shorter free history window. Confirm the applicable
terms, free-plan coverage and two actual S63 sessions before integration.
