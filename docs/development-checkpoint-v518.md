# VALUE HUNTER AI v518

The private Android build ranks ordinary Japanese stocks costing up to
JPY 100,000 per standard lot first, then includes one-share candidates
under the same budget and valuation filters. The results and alerts
show their purchase basis. Broker support for fractional-lot orders
varies. An upgrade rebuilds the alert baseline on the next successful
scan, avoiding alerts for all newly included one-share candidates.

Live authentication, background notifications on a phone and US/SGX
connections still need verification or implementation.
